The previous v2 review is rejected. Four user-reported failures were recorded before new work in outputs/the-haunted-realm-navigation-redesign-brief-v3.md.

## First new iron/glass candidate

Genuine through-object transparency, increased visible scenery, identity distinct from title, readable neutral labels, no prohibited imagery, and expansion cue passed review. Both protected sources and exact title pixels were verified unchanged.

A single localized material-artwork edit added asymmetric aged-glass perimeter fractures, fine corner cobwebs, condensation and irregular cold reflections. Pane centres remain clear; original hardware candidate remains preserved. Protected background/title never became generation targets. The new frame artwork is reflected in different orientations across entries to vary damage placement; lettering, illustration slots, selected illumination and scene remain fixed.

## Final comparison against rejected v2

- Openness: pass. Background is visible through destination glass and reserved image spaces, as well as between objects. No opaque backing panel.
- More visible scenery: pass. The average background blend contribution across the same left-navigation region rises from 52.06% in v2 to 72.59% in the final review. The unobstructed glass layer itself transmits 86.67% of the background. These are alpha-compositing measurements, not optical laboratory measurements.
- Distinct identity: pass. Black corroded iron, clear fractured glass and slim chains replace opaque oak/bronze plaques. No title ornaments or crest were copied.
- Haunted atmosphere: visual review found the cold fractures, cobwebs and neglected iron visibly more haunted. This remains a restrained horror treatment; the user's personal judgment and approval are still required.
- Physical material realism: visual review found believable rust, fasteners, glass edge catches and weathered chains. Asymmetric damage placements reduce the earlier repeated pattern.
- Readability: pass at the 1536 x 1024 review size. Opaque ivory lettering and tight dark keylines remain clear across both versions; the current Home/Activity 3 entry uses warm amber emphasis. Labels and image spaces are unclipped.
- Prohibited imagery: pass. No emojis, cartoon illustrations, clip-art or final activity icons/images were created. IMAGE slots are labeled temporary placeholders.
- Expandability: concept retained. Activities/More activities cues show access to a longer list; temporary visible entries do not fix the total activity count. No functional behaviour was implemented.
- Protected work: all five recorded source/backup hashes match. Zero changed pixels in the protected title rectangle and zero changes outside each navigation overlay. Exact background files, title design, scale and position remain unchanged.

Only static review PNGs were created. Navigation remains NOT APPROVED and no website interface, navigation code, activity page or activity card has been implemented.
