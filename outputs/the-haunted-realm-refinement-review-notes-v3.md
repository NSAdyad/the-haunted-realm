# The Haunted Realm — unapproved visual refinement v3

This is a static review candidate only. It does not implement the Landing Page, navigation, activities, transitions or responsive layout. It does not approve or protect the activity imagery.

## Scope and preserved work

The existing three-by-three composition, column centres, row positions, activity-name centres and maximum scene slots are retained. The exact approved Home navigation reference provides the base, including its temporary navigation labels and IMAGE slots. All nine activity names are rendered directly from the locked wording, rather than generated inside artwork.

The five broadly acceptable scene layers — THE CURSED QUEST, FASTEST FINGER FIRST, WORDS OF THE FEAST, THE PHANTOM ORDER and BUILD THE HAUNTED BANQUET — reproduce their prior resting image layers pixel for pixel. Their name lettering has changed as requested.

The eleven protected source files have their original recorded SHA-256 values before and after this operation. The final page comparison shows zero altered pixels in the protected title rectangle, the entire navigation band, the area above the activity overlays, and outside the new review overlays. The protected background was neither regenerated nor edited; the new scenes and lettering are separate review overlays. Backups and earlier candidates remain preserved.

## Four scene revisions

- ECHOES OF THE PAST now shows ancient roundhouses, a rural path and a cool, misty landscape. Warmth is limited to small doorway light. There is no sunrise and no depicted ritual. Building-form inspiration is the archaeology-informed [Bryn Eryr Iron Age roundhouses at Museum Wales](https://museum.wales/collections/historic-buildings/3/Bryn-Eryr-Iron-Age-Roundhouses/). This evocative artwork is not a factual reconstruction of a particular Samhain ceremony or historical event.
- THE BOOK OF SHADOWS retains a single worn open book and lifted page, with surrounding vessels, herbs, crystal, candle and book-stack props removed. Warmth comes from off-scene light; shadows and mist supply the supernatural atmosphere.
- DOOR TO DARKNESS has a stronger foreground threshold, reception point, dining room and nested architectural perspective. The receding floor and arches make the inward route clearer. The bill/departure detail is small at the all-nine scale, so it should not be read as a complete instructional diagram of every customer-journey stage.
- HOTEL TRANSYLVANIA strengthens the original monster-hotel interpretation through conspicuous supernatural travellers, the concierge, keys and luggage. No movie frame, logo or recognizable film character is used. The generated wolf guest has a full realistic wolf head rather than the initially requested subtler wolf features. Some details of the unapproved hotel architecture, furniture and lighting also shifted. These are design revisions to review imagery, not changes to protected assets or controlled animation.

## Activity lettering

All names share Castelar engraved capitals with directional cut-edge depth, weathering, restrained reflected colour, a spectral impression and localized mist. The lettering uses no wooden plaque, physical rectangular label, blood drips or novelty Halloween font. Cooler aged light is used for ancient/shadow/phantom/depth destinations, sharper light for the buzzer destination, and restrained warm light for dining/preparation/hotel destinations. The exact wording is checked in the raster-authoring source.

The separate focus study uses cropped lettering details with the same scene artwork, glyph geometry, weathering pattern and mist geometry. Only lettering luminance and localized mist/light opacity change. It is a static, subtle lighting study, not working animation or a complete scene comparison. Previous/revised scene pairs are editorial design comparisons and must never be presented as final motion.

## Readability and device scope

The desktop/projector-sized composition is 1536 × 1024. Its lettering is authored at 29 pixels, with fixed centres and a two-line BUILD THE HAUNTED BANQUET name. The separate 320-pixel lettering-only proof reflows long names into two lines at 22 pixels rather than shrinking the complete desktop page. It does not resize, reposition or adapt the protected main title or navigation. It is not a proposed mobile Landing Page.

Visual inspection covers the native raster page, enlarged comparison crops and the small-screen lettering proof. There has been no browser implementation or physical classroom-projector test. Actual device layout, focus interactions and projection visibility remain implementation-stage checks after the user's visual approval and separate authorization.
