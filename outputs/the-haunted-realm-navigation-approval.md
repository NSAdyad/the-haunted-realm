## Protected navigation appearance and concept

- Horizontal individual destinations across the Landing/Home Page; vertical individual destinations at the left of activity pages.
- Corroded iron framing, aged/transparent glass, slender chains, fractured/weathered details and fine cobwebs.
- Open construction: village scenery remains visible through glass and between navigation elements. No large opaque menu backplate, black sidebar replacement or competing full-screen background.
- Clear readable lettering. Keep lettering and iron readable/solid while glass remains transmissive; do not make the entire assembly faint.
- Warm amber/gold emphasis and localized illumination for the currently selected destination, consistent with the approved reference treatment.
- A distinct identity from the protected Ancient Village Sign title; do not turn navigation back into miniature oak/bronze title plaques.
- Realistic, cinematic, physically believable haunted materials and atmosphere.
- The general expandable Home/activity destination concept and complete-list/continuation access remain part of the approved system.

The Home reference has navigation overlay bounds (16,8)-(1520,57); the activity reference has bounds (16,16)-(296,967), on the 1536 x 1024 canvas. These identify the approved mock-up presentation. Implementation and any technical adaptation have not been authorized by this approval.

## Mandatory future activity visual requirement

Each individual activity must eventually receive its own **unique realistic thematic visual identifier**. Base it specifically on that activity's approved information, content and theme, plus the user's corresponding visual references. It must communicate the actual activity and fit naturally into The Haunted Realm.

The required standard is realistic, cinematic, atmospheric, physically believable, professionally integrated, and haunted/spooky where appropriate. The identifier may depict an activity-related object, scene, symbol, environment, character/detail or composition when supported by that activity's approved information.

Prohibited: emojis, emoji-style graphics, cartoons, clip-art, flat vector icons, generic app icons and random symbols unrelated to the activity. Do not substitute generic stock symbols for activity-specific identifiers.

Create visuals one activity at a time, only after the user supplies its corresponding information:

2. Propose the appropriate visual concept.
3. Show the proposed/generated visual.
4. Stop for the user's inspection.
5. Only after explicit approval may it replace that activity's temporary IMAGE space.

Approval of this navigation design does not approve any future activity visual.
